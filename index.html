RSVP Speed Reader

Upload a PDF or paste text and read it one word at a time, Spritz-style, with the optimal recognition point fixed in red so your eyes never move. Adjustable speed, punctuation-aware pacing and speed-aware rewind. Runs entirely in your browser.

Polar H10 ECG Log Analyser

Load an EDF export from the Polar ECG Analysis app and get interactive ECG strips, heart rate, HRV (time, frequency and nonlinear domain), QRS / QT morphology, ectopic beats and cross-day trends. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
